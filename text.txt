The sun rose softly, painting the sky in shades of gold and peach.
The world seemed half asleep, wrapped in silence except for the gentle whisper of the wind.
A single bird sang from the tallest tree, as if reminding everyone that a new day had begun.

Somewhere, a girl sat by her window with a warm cup of tea.
She wasn’t rushing anywhere.
For the first time in a long while, she just breathed — deeply and freely.
The quiet morning didn’t ask her to be strong, or perfect, or busy.
It only asked her to be present.
